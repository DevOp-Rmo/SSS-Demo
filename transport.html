Transport Guidelines

Safety Standards & Traffic Action Plans

Junior Transport Policy

We place the highest priority on the safe and orderly travel of our primary section students. The school actively coordinates safety drills and partners with compliance monitors to ensure children reach the campus securely.

Important Disclaimer: Shri Shikshayatan School does not own, operate, or officially register school buses or carpools. Commute systems are entirely organized by parents/guardians, and the school has no direct control over private transport operations.
  • Safety compliance: Parents/guardians are urged to verify the road-worthiness and legal permits of private vehicles they engage.
  • School Support: Guardians interested in tying up with private pool-car owners or bus providers can consult the school's safety compliance officer.

Senior Transport Policy

Commute guidelines for our senior students emphasize road awareness, punctuality, and cooperation with local traffic authorities. We work together with guardians to ease traffic around the school zones.

Important Disclaimer: Shri Shikshayatan School does not own, operate, or officially register school buses or carpools. Commute systems are entirely organized by parents/guardians, and the school has no direct control over private transport operations.
  • Verification: Guardians should verify driver credentials, commercial license validations, and background checks of private transport operators.
  • Advisory: Senior students are expected to follow designated street lanes when boarding or disembarking private coaches.

Traffic Congestion Action Plan

To ensure minimal disruption to the neighborhood and maximum safety during rush hours, the school implements a strict traffic control roadmap:

  • No Parking Zones: Active on Lord Sinha Road and Pretoria Street from 7:00 AM to 8:30 AM, and 1:30 PM to 3:00 PM.
  • Pretoria Street Rear Gate: Utilized for student access to divide traffic loads.
  • Alphabetical Dispersal: Starting at 2:30 PM, students are dismissed alphabetically based on their respective bus routes (A, B, C, D, etc.).
  • Chartered Bus Terminus: Designated parking slots are coordinated on Pretoria Street, AJC Bose Road, and Theatre Road.
