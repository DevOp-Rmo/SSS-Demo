Shri Shikshayatan School

History

"तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय"

TAMASO MA JYOTIR GAMAYA – Lead us from darkness to light

Founding and Development

Echoing the above motto, Shri Shikshayatan School was founded on January 3, 1954 to empower education amongst young women by enlightening and engaging their bright minds to foster a progressive nation.

In the social scenario of 1920, there was a need to popularize the concept of educating young girls by empowering them with knowledge and quality education. With this vision in mind, a small but progressive school, Marwari Balika Vidyalaya was established at Burrabazar under the stewardship of Shri Ghanshyamdas Birla and Shri Jugal Kishore Birla along with a body of enlightened members of the Rajasthani community settled in Kolkata.

Later the society was named “Shikshayatan Foundation” and today under the guidance of this body, four educational institutions find their presence:

Logo & Symbolism

A lamp has been placed as our logo – “Prajwalito Dyanamay Padeepaha” – which means “a lamp of knowledge has been lit and that all may bathe in its sight”. The blue outline is the indication that the sky is the limit.

Campus & Trustees

Shri Shikshayatan School shares a common campus with Shri Shikshayatan College. Both these institutions have earned acclaim over the years to emerge as premier educational institutions in Kolkata owing to the relentless efforts of Late Sitaram Seksaria and Late Bhagirathmal Kanoria – two of the esteemed trustees of the society.
