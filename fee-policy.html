Policy

Fee Payment Policy

  • Online Payment Only — School fees must be paid exclusively through online modes. No cash or offline counter payments are accepted.
  • Accepted Online Modes — Debit Card, Credit Card, or Net Banking via the School's website www.shrishikshayatanschool.com with NIL transaction charges.
  • Bank Transfer NOT Accepted — Payments through RTGS / NEFT / IMPS or any direct bank-to-school transfer will not be accepted under any circumstances.
  • Quarterly Schedule — Fees are structured on a quarterly basis. Each quarter must be cleared by the respective due date listed in the schedule below.
Parents can access the ERP Portal to pay fees, check payment receipts, or download annual fee statements by logging in at erp.shrishikshayatanschool.com.

Refund

Refund Policy

Fees once paid are generally non-refundable. Refunds may be considered only in exceptional circumstances such as:
  • Duplicate payments
  • Other reasons approved by School Management
Refund requests must be submitted in writing with proof of payment.
Admission fee is non-refundable under any circumstances. This applies to all classes and sections regardless of the reason for withdrawal.
In case a student leaves the school due to:
  • Transfer of parent
  • Health reasons
  • Any other unforeseen circumstances
A pro rata (proportional) return of quarterly/term/annual fees shall be made if the fees are paid in advance. However, if classes have commenced and the student did not attend for their own reasons before withdrawal, the School will not be liable to refund the fee for those months.
If a guardian wishes to withdraw their ward mid-session, they must:
  • Submit a written application 30 days in advance
  • Clear all fees up to the month their ward last attended (if not already paid in advance)
Approved refunds will be processed through the original mode of payment within 15–30 working days of approval. The School reserves the right to modify the Refund Policy at any time.
