Kalabhavan

A Sanctuary for Visual Arts, Performance, and Music

Nurturing Creativity and Expression

Kalabhavan is the creative heartbeat of Shri Shikshayatan School. Designed as a dedicated multi-disciplinary academy for fine arts, crafts, and performing arts, it offers our students the space and guidance to discover their artistic passions and channel their emotional expression.

Equipped with specialized acoustic setups, classical instrument cabinets, visual art studios, and dedicated dance floors, Kalabhavan provides training under certified art mentors, instrumentalists, and music exponents.

Students participate in sketching, clay modeling, pottery, vocal training, sitar/keyboard practice, and classical dance forms. The center works to preserve cultural heritage while encouraging modern creative innovations.

  • Fine Arts & Crafts Studios for clay modeling, canvas painting, and sketching.
  • Acoustically treated Music Rooms containing synthesizers, guitars, sitars, and tables.
  • Spacious Performing Arts rooms equipped with full-length mirrors and safety dance floors.
