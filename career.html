Official Recruitment Circular

Inviting Applications from Experienced Teachers

Shri Shikshayatan School is looking for passionate, dynamic, and techno-savvy educators committed to academic excellence and holistic student development under the CBSE curriculum.

Physics, Biology, Chemistry

Qualification: M.Sc. with B.Ed.

English, Hindi

Qualification: M.A. with B.Ed.

Financial Markets Management

Qualification: M.Com. / MBA / M.A. (Economics) with B.Ed.

Fashion Studies

Qualification: Bachelor's Degree in Fashion Design / Apparel Construction or equivalent from Govt. recognized Institute / University with B.Ed.

Primary Teachers

Qualification: Graduate / Post Graduate with D.El.Ed.

Western Dance Part-Time

Qualification: Diploma / Certificate in Western Dance from a recognized institution with 2–3 years of teaching experience in a school or dance academy.

How to Apply

Candidates must possess the requisite subject qualifications and a recognized professional teaching degree. Interested educators are invited to email their updated CV along with recent photograph and credentials to:

careers@shrishikshayatanschool.com

Please mention the post applied for in the subject line of your email.
