Aggregate Marks Computation System

For evaluation, the school will compute class X marks (from PA II / Half Yearly / Mid Term) as follows:

  • Group I: Sum of English, Second Language, Mathematics, Science (Physics + Chemistry + Biology) and Social Science (History + Geography + Political Science + Economics).
  • Group II & Group III (with Math/Econ): Sum of English, Mathematics, and best three scores (excluding additional subjects).
  • Group III (without Math/Econ): Sum of English and best four scores (excluding additional subjects).

Written Examination Syllabus Details

English50 Marks
  • Unseen Comprehension (20M): Two passages of ~500 words each (VSAQ and MCQ, no options).
  • Letter to the Editor (8M): Word limit 120-150 words.
  • Paragraph Writing (10M): Word limit 150-200 words.
  • Grammar (12M): Subject-Verb Concord, Articles, Tenses, Narration, Active/Passive Voice, Phrases, Clauses.
Physics17 Marks
  • Electricity: Current, potential difference, Ohm's law, resistance, resistivity, series & parallel circuits, heating effects, electric power.
  • Magnetic Effects: Field lines, current-carrying solenoid, force on conductor, Fleming's LHR, domestic circuits.
  • Light: Reflection/refraction at spherical surfaces, mirror/lens formulas, magnification, refraction through prism, dispersion.
  • Mechanics: Newton's Laws of Motion, force, momentum, inertia, action-reaction.
Chemistry17 Marks
  • Chemical Reactions: Equations, balancing, and types of reactions.
  • Acids, Bases & Salts: General properties, indicators, and chemical reactions.
  • Metals & Non-Metals: Physical/chemical properties, metallurgy, extraction and refining.
  • Organic Chemistry: Basic concepts, nomenclature, homologous series, carbon compounds, soaps & detergents.
Biology16 Marks
  • Life Processes: Photosynthesis, respiration, transport, excretion in plants and humans.
  • Reproduction: DNA copying, variations, sexual reproduction in humans, reproductive health.
  • Heredity: Mendelian monohybrid cross.
  • Environment: Global warming, ozone layer depletion.
Math (General)50 Marks
  • Quadratic Equations, Rational/Irrational Numbers.
  • Mensuration (excluding frustum of cone).
  • Theorems on Circles, Trigonometry & heights/distances.
  • Similarity, Simultaneous Linear Equations.
  • Factorization, Coordinate Geometry.
  • Powers & Exponents, Statistics, Profit & Loss, Interest.
Math (Aptitude)50 Marks
  • Mensuration, Simultaneous Equations.
  • Coordinate Geometry, Statistics (excluding Ogive).
  • Profit & Loss, Percentage, Unitary Method.
  • Simple Interest, Ratio & Proportion, Decimals & Fractions, Average.

Steps to Successfully Fill Up the Application Form

1
Click on the 'ONLINE ADMISSION' option available on the Home page of the School Website or directly access the registration portal: Admission Tree Portal .
2
Click on the 'Apply Now' button (marked as Admission Going On | Apply Now).
3
Create your account and fill up applicant details as per the fields shown.
4
Proceed to transaction page and make the Online Payment.
5
An auto-generated confirmation email will be sent to your registered Email ID from Admission Tree.
6
Open the email and click the link to complete the detailed school application form:
  • a) Create a School Login ID and Password on the school portal.
  • b) A unique Application Number will be generated.
  • c) Upload documents using the School ERP Portal: School ERP Portal .
7
Take a physical printout of the completed Application Form and submit it inside the School Drop Box at the gate with necessary documents within the deadline.
