Sporting Facilities

A Dynamic Arena for Physical Fitness and Team Spirit

Sports and Physical Development

Physical fitness is vital for shaping disciplined, resilient, and active young individuals. Shri Shikshayatan School offers premium, spacious indoor game halls and sprawling outdoor fields to accommodate a wide spectrum of athletic disciplines.

We have specialized instructors on campus to train students in swimming, aerobics, martial arts (Karate), and traditional drill activities. Our students actively represent the school in CBSE national sports events, state-level swimming competitions, and basketball tournaments.

Our sports infrastructure is designed with advanced shock-absorbing safety tiles, child-safe barriers, and complete medical supervision to prevent injury and promote healthy sportsmanship.

  • Indoor swimming pool with certified lifeguards and clean water systems.
  • Spacious outdoor courts for basketball, throwball, and drill drills.
  • Indoor arenas for Aerobics, Karate (Martial Arts), Yoga, and Table Tennis.
