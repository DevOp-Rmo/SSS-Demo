Foundation Day

Celebrating Our Legacy, Vision, and Institutional Heritage

Honoring the visionary milestones of Shri Shikshayatan School in fostering quality education since 1920.

Foundation Day marks the historic anniversary of the establishment of Shri Shikshayatan School. Commemorating the visionary efforts of our founder members who pioneered and championed girls' education in Kolkata, this day is celebrated with deep gratitude and pride. The annual celebrations feature traditional oil lamp lighting, choral assemblies, stage enactments, and inspiring messages from the Board of Trustees and guest speakers, tracing our glorious path of empowering girl children.
