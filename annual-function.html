Annual Function

Celebrating Talents, Achievements, and Cultural Expressions

The Junior Department Annual Prize Day is a vibrant celebration of our young students' artistic, theatrical, and academic accomplishments over the school session. Through high-energy stage plays, cultural dance enactments, and musical performances, students showcase their creative expressions before parents and staff.

The Senior Annual Concert & Prize Distribution Ceremony is the pinnacle cultural event for the Senior Department of Shri Shikshayatan School. It honors the students who have scaled outstanding peaks in academics, co-curricular tournaments, and community engagement.
