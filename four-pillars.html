The Four Pillars

Ethos and Foundations of Shri Shikshayatan School

Moulding students into confident, compassionate global citizens ready to meet the challenges of the tomorrow.

Upholding the social, intellectual, and value-creating ethos of UNESCO to be able to live together in cultural awareness, to learn to comprehend, analyse, assess, and embody the ability to research, deduce, and empower knowledge, the four pillars of Shri Shikshayatan School serve to aid the enhancement of each student.

Pillar I

Values

Values are integrated and imparted through everyday teaching, fostering ethical standards, cultural awareness, and moral integrity in every student's daily life.

Pillar II

Understanding

Understanding is accepting the oneness of humanity, harbouring love for fellow human beings, and cultivating deep respect for all living things.

Pillar III

Excellence

Excellence in all things is striving for perfection and beauty, learning to do one's best always, and continuously scaling new heights in all academic and creative endeavors.

Pillar IV

Service

Service to the world goes beyond simple charity. We recognize service as a conscious way of life, so that children grow up to become leaders of thought and action.
