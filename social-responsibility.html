Values in Action

School Social Responsibility

Cultivating compassion, empathy, and active citizenship. Through dedicated social service, our students learn the value of giving back to society.

Our Vision

Nurturing Compassionate Changemakers

At Shri Shikshayatan School, education extends beyond textbook learning. We believe that true leadership lies in service to others. Our School Social Responsibility (SSR) program is designed to integrate values of charity, civic responsibility, and community partnership into the everyday life of our students.

By engaging directly with social and environmental issues, students develop crucial life skills including interpersonal communication, stress management, and a deep appreciation for the diverse community around them.

Key Initiatives

How We Serve the Community

Our students participate in structured outreach programs throughout the academic year, addressing critical needs in primary education, support for seniors, and environmental sustainability.

Hope Kolkata Foundation & Daan Utsav

We work in close collaboration with the Hope Kolkata Foundation. Every year, students participate in seasonal charity campaigns to bring cheer to underprivileged children.

  • Daan Utsav: The joy of giving donation festival.
  • #GiftswithHOPE: Distributing new clothes and festival gifts.

Empowering the Girl Child

Dedicated programs aimed at fostering equal educational access, organizing mentoring camps, and extending scholarships to ensure no girl is left behind due to financial hurdles.

  • Sponsoring resources for municipal schools.
  • Peer-to-peer mentoring workshops.

Outreach for the Elderly

Developing intergenerational connections. Students regularly visit and spend time with residents of local old age homes, providing companionship and material support.

  • Visiting local senior living facilities.
  • Fundraisers for medical aids and blankets.

Road Safety Advocacy

Campaigns and seminars in collaboration with local traffic authorities. Students help spread awareness about defensive driving, safety signs, and pedestrian safety.

  • Peer-led street campaigns.
  • Workshops with Kolkata Police officers.

"Be Someone's Santa" Competition

An innovative initiative combining entrepreneurship and social charity. Student teams manufacture and market handmade items, donating 100% of profits to child welfare NGOs.

  • Handmade product design challenges.
  • Direct business exposure for fundraising.

Go Green Campaign

Environmental welfare through tree plantation drives, waste recycling projects, and plastic-free campus drives conducted by the school's Eco Club.

  • Annual plantation activities.
  • Waste segregation and composting projects.
