Head Mistress's Message

Mrs. Poushali Mukherjee

Headmistress (Primary Section)

Shri Shikshayatan School

“Lead me from darkness to light…”

The eternal prayer is our motto. Saluting the idea of our founder members of breaking down the barriers to girls education – we in the primary section of Shri Shikshayatan School, design our curriculum to motivate every child to believe that no achievement is impossible.

With a wide range of activities, we help them to dream of a better tomorrow by liberating their minds – as we believe that learning is always reciprocal. With a perfect blend of tech-aided learning through smart classrooms, language labs, tablet usage and value based education for character building, our extremely competent and educated staff – all together, enable the little girl to walk ahead confidently in her search for excellence.

Poushali Mukherjee Mrs. Poushali Mukherjee Headmistress, Primary Section
