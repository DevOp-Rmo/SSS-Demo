Fee Payment Instructions

  • Quarterly Schedules: School fees must be cleared on a quarterly basis. • Quarter 1: April - June
    • Quarter 2: July - September
    • Quarter 3: October - December
    • Quarter 4: January - March
  • ERP Online Payments: All fees are payable strictly online via Net Banking, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, or UPI using the parent login portal.
  • Late Payments: Fees must be cleared within the designated due dates of the first month of every quarter. Late fees will be levied thereafter as per school policies.
