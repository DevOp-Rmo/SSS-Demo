Outreach & Excursion

Global Collaboration and Intercultural exchange programs

Shri Shikshayatan School has a collaboration with a number of countries.

The school is in partnership with many countries like USA, UK, Europe and many others. Our school has moved globally after receiving the prestigious International School Award in 2009, 2013 and 2017. Shri Shikshayatan aims to make its students true “Global Citizens” with a concern towards the environment and global issues that are economic, social, political and cultural in nature and are common across borders.
