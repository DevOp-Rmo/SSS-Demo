Career Counselling

Nurturing Aspirations, Shaping Futures

Empowering Students for Global Opportunities

Welcome to the Career Counselling Cell

This cell’s primary object is to service the students with appropriate guidance to establish linkage with the world of work and locate career and academic opportunities.

It also offers information on professional development courses and opportunities with regard to higher studies in India and abroad. Extensive career counseling and career guidance programmes are organized where prominent personalities from various walks of life by the school through renowned organizations, conduct programmes on career counseling at different levels.

Core Pillars of Our Guidance Cell

Career Exploration & Guidance

Providing comprehensive resources and counseling sessions to help students map their interests to practical career goals and choices.

Higher Studies Assistance

In-depth information and direct application support for prestigious universities and higher studies pathways across India and international destinations.

Professional Development

Guidance on vocational streams, industry courses, and professional development programs to build essential workplace skill sets.

Expert Seminars & Panels

Regular interaction programs and panel discussions featuring prominent figures from diverse professional walks of life and renowned institutions.

"The best way to predict the future is to create it. We stand committed to guiding our students towards careers that inspire them."
