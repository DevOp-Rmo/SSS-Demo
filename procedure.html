Admission Policy (Classes VI - IX)

All admissions are strictly merit-based and subject to the availability of vacancies in each respective class. Shri Shikshayatan School follows a transparent and streamlined admission workflow.

01

Online Application Submission

All applications must be submitted online through the school website. Parents must accurately fill in the candidate's personal, academic, and guardian details.

02

Shortlisting & Admit Cards

Submitted applications are evaluated. Eligible candidates are shortlisted for admission tests, and notice boards are updated with dates.

03

Admission Tests

Shortlisted candidates appear for admission tests. These evaluate core capabilities in languages, mathematics, and science appropriate to the target class level.

04

Result Publication

A list of selected candidates is compiled based on exam performance and published on the School Notice Board and official website.

05

Guardian & Student Interview

Selected candidates and their parents/legal guardians must attend an interview. Please bring all original documentation and academic reports.

06

Admission Completion

Upon successful verification of documents and clearance of admission fees, enrollment is finalized, and school material schedules are issued.

Provisional Admission Policy (Class XI)

Class XI admission forms are hosted on our online portal. Admissions are provisional, finalized post publication of Class X Board results.

01

Form Download & Application

Access forms via the online admission portal. Select preferred streams (Science, Commerce, or Humanities) and elective subjects.

02

Admission Test & Interview

Shortlisted external candidates must appear for an admission test. Internal students are admitted provisionally based on their school pre-board evaluation.

03

Selection List & Document Review

Results are uploaded online. Selected students and their parents attend an interview for elective evaluation.

04

Final Admission Confirmation

Provisional selections are formally confirmed after submitting official Class X Board Marksheets, Migration Certificates, and Character certificates.
