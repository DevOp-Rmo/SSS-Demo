Important Instructions & Conditions

  • Online payment for the Application Form fee is Rs. 1,000/- (convenience fees apply additionally).
  • The official prospectus is available directly on the school's website.
  • Buying the Application Form does not assure or guarantee selection for the interview procedure.
  • Application form-fill procedure will only be complete after successful Online Payment is made, documents are uploaded, and the physical printout is submitted in the drop box.
  • Date of Issue of Birth Certificate: Must be completed within one year of birth. Candidates whose birth certificates were issued more than one year after their birth will not be eligible for the interview.

Steps to Successfully Fill Up the Application Form

1
Click on the 'ONLINE ADMISSION' option available on the Home page of the School Website or directly access the registration portal: Admission Tree Portal .
2
Click on the 'Apply Now' button (marked as Admission Going On | Apply Now).
3
Create your account and fill up the initial applicant and parent details as per the fields shown.
4
Proceed to the checkout/transaction page and complete the Online Payment.
5
An auto-generated confirmation email will be sent to your registered Email ID from Admission Tree.
6
Open the email and click the link to complete the detailed school application form:
  • a) You will be prompted to create a School Login ID and Password. Store these securely.
  • b) A unique Application Number will be generated for all future school communications.
  • c) Upload all required documents by editing your online application within the stipulated deadline at the School ERP Portal: School ERP Portal .
7
Take a physical printout of the completed Application Form and submit it inside the School Drop Box along with all necessary documents listed in the form within the deadline.
